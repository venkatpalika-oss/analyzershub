Troubleshooting & Procedures

Field-oriented guidance covering operation, calibration, maintenance, and shared AZ alarms.

Overview

The Endura AZ25 uses a zirconium oxide cell at the probe tip for direct measurement in a furnace or flue duct. It is not a TDLAS analyzer.

Calibration

Match calibration to the installed AZ25 probe and transmitter documentation. The product overview describes test-gas calibration; it does not support a separate generic “reference gas calibration” step or the former failure-frequency claim.

Maintenance

Routine maintenance improves reliability and reduces nuisance alarms.

  • Inspect sensor condition and response time
  • Verify heater temperature stability
  • Check sample filters and flow
  • Confirm reference gas/air path is clean and dry
  • Inspect wiring and grounding
