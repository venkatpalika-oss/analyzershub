Troubleshooting & Procedures

Practical field reference: overview, calibration, maintenance, and shared AZ alarms.

Overview

The Endura AZ20 system uses a zirconia combustion oxygen probe with a transmitter. Select the probe, transmitter and accessories against the documented process limits; no relative accuracy ranking is established here.

Calibration

Follow the transmitter calibration instructions for the installed test-gas arrangement. The probe reference-air supply and test-gas connections serve separate functions; “reference gas calibration” is not an additional generic calibration step.

Match gas values, connections and any automatic calibration options to the probe and transmitter manuals.

Maintenance

Inspect sensors, heaters, and reference gas/air path periodically. Replace components as recommended by the manufacturer.

  • Inspect the fitted probe protection and gas connections according to the probe manual
  • Confirm heater temperature stability (no hunting)
  • Verify reference/air path is clean and dry
  • Check wiring/earth for noise issues
