Rosemount 6888 / 6888A — Zirconia Oxygen Analyzer

In-situ oxygen measurement in combustion flue gas. The technical notes on this page refer specifically to the Rosemount 6888A manual, Rev AB.

Decision Trees → Alarm Reference → ← Back to Search

Analyzer Overview

The 6888A places a heated zirconia sensor in the flue-gas duct or stack. Its cell voltage follows the logarithm of the oxygen partial-pressure ratio between reference gas and measured gas.

Key Features

  • Zirconia oxygen sensor with integrated heater
  • Accuracy and response depend on the documented configuration and operating conditions
  • Match probe and temperature options to the application limits in section 1.7
  • Supports field calibration and onboard diagnostics

Typical Applications

  • Boilers and fired heaters
  • Use in a monitoring system does not by itself establish regulatory compliance
  • Combustion control and efficiency optimization
  • Industrial furnaces and incinerators

Maintenance & Calibration Notes

  • Allow heater temperature to fully stabilize before trusting readings
  • Use the configuration-matched calibration gases. The cited manual rejects pure nitrogen as the low-point gas
  • Inspect wiring, heater circuits, and electronics periodically
  • Check zirconia sensor for contamination, cracking, or aging
Sections 1.7 and 3.6 of the cited manual differ on instrument air as high calibration gas. Manufacturer documentation requires configuration-specific verification; no definitive instrument-air calibration procedure is given here. Reference air and calibration gas have separate functions.

A calibration-gas response test is not the same as response to a process change; gas velocity and diffuser condition affect installed response.
