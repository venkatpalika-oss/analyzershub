Yokogawa OX400 — Low Concentration Zirconia Oxygen Analyzer

Low-concentration zirconia oxygen analyzer for sampled gas, including oxygen in nitrogen, argon and helium. Configuration details below follow GS 11M10B01-01E.

← Back to Search Yokogawa Decision Trees Hub → OX400 Documentation Notes → Alarm Documentation →
OX400 is a sampled-gas instrument. The former boiler-probe, flange and purge guidance on this page did not describe the documented product.

Measurement and sampling

A heated zirconia sensor measures oxygen in gas supplied to the analyzer. The documented sample-drawing options are a built-in pump, an aspirator, or no suction device. Match sample supply and outlet conditions to the ordered option.

This documentation does not establish suitability as a direct-insertion boiler flue-gas probe.

Configuration and outputs

The specification lists analog outputs and RS-232 communication. Check the installed model code and wiring documentation; Modbus/Ethernet and boiler mounting-flange or purge-system claims are not supported here.

Calibration and service

Use the OX400 instructions matched to the installed range and sample-drawing option. A generic zirconia-probe zero/span sequence is not supplied here. Confirm gas suitability, sample connections and operating conditions before interpreting a fault.
