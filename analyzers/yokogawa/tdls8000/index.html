Yokogawa TDLS8000 Gas Analyzer

Analyzer Overview

TDLS8000 scans a tunable laser across a gas absorption line to determine concentration along the optical path. The installed gas channel, path length, pressure and temperature settings must match the application.

Key Features

  • In-situ cross-path measurement or a documented flow-cell sampling arrangement, depending on configuration
  • Gas species and simultaneous measurements depend on the ordered channels and options
  • Analysis-cycle time alone does not establish installed system response
  • A path measurement does not by itself demonstrate representative whole-duct composition

Typical Applications

  • Combustion gas monitoring
  • Emissions and process gas analysis
  • Boilers, furnaces, and kilns
  • Environmental monitoring

Maintenance & Calibration Notes

  • Check laser output and optical alignment
  • Maintain sufficient optical transmission; particles, dirty windows and misalignment can reduce it. There is no universal dust-free requirement or particulate threshold here
  • Follow configuration-specific validation/calibration instructions, including optical path and purge conditions
  • Inspect electronics module periodically
